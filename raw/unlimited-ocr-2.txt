Pioneering the OCR-3.0 Era: From Long- to Unlimited-Horizon Parsing

Abstract
Unlimited OCR ushers the era of long-horizon parsing, substantially mitigating the growth of
working memory as document lengthens. However, as document length scales to hundreds
of pages, the visual context grows substantially, sharply increasing the computational cost
of each decoding step and limiting the scalability of ultra-long document parsing. Inspired
by human book transcription via localized focus without re-scanning preceding pages, we
introduce Unlimited OCR 2, a document parsing model that looks only at what it transcribes.
Building on Unlimited OCR, we upgrade all the decoder attention modules to Sparse Reference
Sliding Window Attention (SR-SWA), bounding active reference tokens to a fixed capacity and
thus sustaining constant computational costs across arbitrarily long documents. By combining
SR-SWA with Unlimited-Horizon Decoding Paradigm, Unlimited OCR 2 enables document
parsing over virtually unlimited lengths without prohibitive memory overhead. At the hundred-
page scale, Unlimited OCR 2 achieves 7.0× and 5.6× per-step speedups over Full Attention and
R-SWA, respectively. Furthermore, it delivers state-of-the-art end-to-end performance on both
MPDocBench and MarathonDocBench, our newly proposed benchmark targeting hundred-
page document parsing. Ultimately, the capacity for uninterrupted, one-shot document parsing
signifies a fundamental paradigm shift into the OCR 3.0 era.

1. Introduction
“Pixels are my body, and tokens are my blood. I have parsed over a thousand pages. Unknown
to KV cache. Nor known to context limits. So, as I pray: Unlimited OCR Works.”
Snarcy on huggingface ( Adapted fromUBW)
Document parsing has undergone a major paradigm shift, transitioning from traditional
multi-stage pipelines (OCR-1.0) [5, 6, 8, 20, 22, 26, 40] with handcrafted detection and recognition
modules to end-to-end Vision-Language Models (OCR-2.0) [7, 18, 19, 21, 30, 31]. By formulat-
ing document understanding as a unified sequence-to-sequence transcription task, OCR-2.0
frameworks effectively eliminate cumulative pipeline errors. Our previous work, Unlimited
OCR [37], pushed OCR-2.0 boundaries to tens of pages by using Reference Sliding Window
Attention (R-SWA) to maintain a constant KV cache. Nevertheless, it still computes attention
over all visual tokens across pages during decoding. As document length scales, this persistent
attention to non-informative visual tokens severely escalates visual redundancy, compounding
the difficulty of end-to-end inference. This visual attention bottleneck naturally prompts a
fundamental question: how can we transition from length-limited parsing to an unlimited
one-shot paradigm and genuinely inaugurate the OCR-3.0 era?
To answer this question, human transcription of lengthy books provides a clear physical
intuition for parsing scaling: as we copy text page by page, we maintain localized visual
focus, casting only momentary glances back when cross-page references are needed, rather
than keeping the entire volume active in mind. This cognitive mechanism reveals that visual
dependencies in multi-page documents are inherently page-sparse yet intra-page concentrated.
However, standard dense global attention completely overlooks this sparse dynamic, incurring
redundant attention interactions across all pages. This not only incurs prohibitive computational
overhead, but also leads to severe memory bottlenecks as document length scales. To break
through these efficiency and memory bottlenecks, we align machine perception with human
reading patterns and present Unlimited OCR 2, pioneering an unlimited-horizon parsing
framework for the OCR-3.0 era. Our main contributions are as follows:
• We introduce Sparse Reference Sliding Window Attention (SR-SWA), a hierarchical sparse
attention mechanism designed for unlimited visual context. Specifically, Page Gate first
narrows each query’s visual reference scope from the entire document to relevant pages
while robustly modeling multi-page positional relationships. Token Sieve selectively in-
corporates top-𝑘 remote token buckets to capture long-range visual dependencies, thereby
drastically reducing attention overhead while preserving comprehensive context.
• We introduce MarathonDocBench, a benchmark for hundred-page document parsing. It
substantially extends existing evaluations in document length and cross-page structural
complexity, enabling a comprehensive assessment of long-document parsing performance
in the hundred-page regime.
• Comprehensive evaluations in long-horizon settings demonstrate that our approach main-
tains constant per-step decoding latency regardless of document length while achieving
state-of-the-art end-to-end performance on multi-page benchmarks. Building on these
advances, we establish an efficient unlimited-horizon parsing paradigm by integrating
SR-SWA with the Unlimited-Horizon Decoding Paradigm, enabling document parsing
over virtually unlimited lengths.
In summary, SR-SWA fundamentally breaks the computational and memory bottlenecks of
ultra-long visual context. Operating at constant per-step decoding latency with length-invariant
accuracy across hundreds of pages, Unlimited OCR 2 unlocks scalable long-document parsing
and paves the way for the OCR-3.0 era of unlimited-horizon understanding.

2. Related Works
2.1. VLM-based Document Parsing
Document parsing has increasingly been reshaped by advances in vision-language models,
evolving from pipeline-based OCR-1.0 systems [5, 6, 8, 20, 22, 26, 40], which combine specialized
page analysis modules with VLM-based decoding, to unified VLM-based OCR-2.0 systems that
directly generate structured representations from document images. OCR-2.0 encompasses both
single-page parsers [7, 18, 19, 21, 30, 31] and Unlimited OCR [37], which extends end-to-end
parsing to multi-page documents by jointly modeling their visual content within a shared
context. However, although Unlimited OCR improves long-document efficiency with R-SWA,
it still densely attends to all remote visual tokens, causing the visual context accessible to
each decoding query to expand with document length and creating a fundamental scalability
bottleneck at the hundred-page scale. Unlimited OCR 2 overcomes this limitation by explicitly
bounding remote visual access, enabling attention-efficient end-to-end parsing of hundred-page
documents and advancing document parsing into the OCR-3.0 era.
2.2. Efficient Long-Context Modeling
Efficient long-context modeling has been explored through both attention replacement and
sparse attention. Linear or recurrent alternatives [11, 15, 17, 35, 36] reduce long-context cost by
summarizing historical information into compact states. Document parsing, however, requires
long-range memory to preserve fine-grained visual evidence for accurate recognition and cross-
page retrieval, which may not be fully retained within such compact representations.
Sparse attention retains direct access to selected historical tokens or KV blocks while avoid-
ing dense all-to-all computation. Early methods use predefined patterns such as local win-
dows and global tokens [2, 38], whereas recent approaches adopt query-dependent retrieval.
Hard-selection methods, such as Compressed Sparse Attention(CSA) [33] and MiniMax Sparse
Attention(MSA) [16], rely on discrete top- 𝑘 routing and typically require auxiliary supervi-
sion or warmup. In contrast, DashAttention [ 13] and HiLS [12] incorporate differentiable or
forward-integrated routing scores, enabling more direct end-to-end optimization.

3.1.2. MarathonDocBench
Existing multi-page document parsing benchmarks neither extend to the hundred-page scale
nor sufficiently capture the rich cross-page structures that emerge in long documents. To
address this gap, we introduce MarathonDocBench, the first benchmark specifically designed
for hundred-page document parsing. It contains 100 documents spanning 103–300 pages with
205k annotated layout elements, extending the document-length range by roughly an order of
magnitude beyond MPDocBench [41].
Beyond document length, MarathonDocBench contains substantially richer cross-page struc-
tures than MPDocBench, with cross-page text and tables appearing much more frequently
and densely across documents, as summarized in Figure 2. This makes MarathonDocBench a
substantially more challenging testbed for evaluating whether end-to-end parsers can preserve
document-level structure while scaling from tens to hundreds of pages. To ensure reliable
evaluation, structural annotations, including bounding boxes, block type, hierarchy, reading
order, and cross-page continuations of text and tables, are initialized from a strong page-by-page
parser and carefully refined by human annotators. Content transcriptions are initialized from
the same parser outputs and verified through an iterative VLM-based refinement pipeline, with
flagged disagreements adjudicated by human annotators.
3.2. Architecture
Unlimited OCR 2 inherits the fundamental backbone from Unlimited OCR, leveraging DeepSeek
OCR [28] as its foundation. Specifically, it employs the DeepEncoder paired with a Mixture-of-
Experts (MoE) decoder architecture featuring 3B total parameters and 500M activated parameters
per token. Building upon this base, we replace the baseline R-SWA with our proposed SR-SWA
as shown in Figure 3. While R-SWA bounds local interactions, its visual context scope still
expands with growing document length, leading to noticeable latency accumulation over ultra-
long horizons. By introducing a selective remote visual routing mechanism, SR-SWA strictly
bounds the active context scope, enabling Unlimited OCR 2 to maintain a constant KV cache
footprint across both text and visual modalities during unlimited-horizon decoding. We detail
the inner mechanics of SR-SWA in the following subsections.

4. Experimental Settings
4.1. Data Engine
To train Unlimited OCR 2 for versatile document parsing, we construct a large-scale multimodal
corpus comprising 2M multi-page and 10M single-page documents. Pseudo-labels across both
single- and multi-page subsets are extracted from MinerU2.5-Pro [ 26] and PaddleOCR-VL-
v1.6 [40], tailored to their respective domain strengths. To enrich visual and layout diversity, we
additionally incorporate versatile synthetic document generation pipelines. Across both real and
synthetic data, we leverage VLM-based multi-dimensional feature analysis to conduct balance
filtering, ensuring uniform coverage over complex layout distributions. Finally, to explicitly
fortify structural hierarchy and cross-page continuity in multi-page scenarios, we apply MinerU-
Popo [34] refinement alongside dedicated heuristic post-processing rules to reconcile layout
inconsistencies across adjacent page boundaries.
4.2. Implementation Details
We initialize Unlimited OCR 2 from the Unlimited OCR checkpoint and perform post-training
for 1 epoch on the curated dataset. Training is conducted on a cluster of 25× 8 GPU NVIDIA
H800 nodes with a global batch size of 1,600 and a maximum sequence length of 64K tokens.

5.2. Main Results
As visually summarized in Figure 6 and further detailed in Table 1 and Table 2, Unlimited
OCR 2 takes a significant step toward the OCR 3.0 era, advancing document parsing along
three key dimensions. First, it establishes the practical feasibility of hundred-page end-to-end
parsing, achieving a score of 77.06 on MarathonDocBench and demonstrating its ability to
handle documents at a scale beyond conventional multi-page settings. Second, it maintains
robust parsing performance as document length increases. Through end-to-end modeling with
SR-SWA, Unlimited OCR 2 filters redundant visual tokens while preserving relevant cross-page
information, achieving the highest Overall scores and state-of-the-art performance on both
cross-page text and table metrics, T-Text and T-Table, respectively, from dozens of pages on
MPDocBench to hundreds of pages on MarathonDocBench. Third, it establishes a new state-
of-the-art in long-document parsing, outperforming leading closed-source models and top-tier
open-source MLLMs across these benchmarks. Furthermore, Unlimited OCR 2 retains strong
single-page recognition capabilities, achieving an Overall score of 94.02% on OmniDocBench in
Table 3, confirming that its sparse attention design preserves fine-grained local recognition.

5.3. Scaling with Document Length
5.3.1. Feasibility of Unlimited-horizon Parsing
Benefiting from SR-SWA and the Unlimited-Horizon Decoding Paradigm, Unlimited OCR 2
enables efficient parsing of extremely long documents under constrained GPU memory. We
evaluate its feasibility on the hundred-page MarathonDocBench benchmark by measuring
peak KV cache memory and end-to-end throughput, as reported in Table 4. Compared with
standard decoding, page-level chunking with boundary-page KV cache reuse reduces peak KV
cache memory from 47.00 GB to 1.77 GB under single-sequence inference (𝐵= 1). Meanwhile,
end-to-end throughput increases from 360 to 1,315 TPS under concurrent inference with𝐵= 16.
These results demonstrate that sequential chunk processing with KV cache reuse substantially
reduces memory requirements while improving throughput, making hundred-page document
parsing more practical under limited GPU memory.
5.3.2. Length-Invariant Accuracy
To verify whether extending the document horizon compromises parsing fidelity, we evaluate the
performance trajectories of Unlimited OCR 2 across continuously increasing document lengths.
As illustrated in Figure 6 (a), frontier Vision-Language Models (VLMs) typically experience a
noticeable performance degradation as the sequence length grows, primarily caused by attention
diffusion and context dilution over ultra-long horizons. In contrast, Unlimited OCR 2 exhibits
remarkable length-invariant accuracy. By leveraging SR-SWA to dynamically isolate and retrieve
only the most relevant visual tokens, our model maintains consistently high parsing precision
from short sequences up to extreme horizons of 300 pages.

5.3.3. Constant Per-Step Decoding Latency
Figure 6 (b) examines how decoding efficiency scales with document length. All measurements
are conducted in Gundam mode with a batch size of 3 and a fixed generation length of 1,024
tokens per page. As the document length grows, Full Attention incurs rapidly growing per-step
decoding latency because each query must attend to a linearly expanding visual context. While
R-SWA mitigates this overhead by bounding local window, its remote visual scope still scales
with sequence length, leading to noticeable latency accumulation over ultra-long horizons. In
contrast, SR-SWA strictly bounds the effective per-query attention scope, achieving near-constant
per-step decoding latency from short contexts up to hundred-page documents. Consequently, its
latency and throughput advantage over Full Attention and naive windowed baselines becomes
increasingly pronounced as document length scales, confirming the computational viability of
our paradigm for real-world long-document parsing.
6. Limitation and Future Work
Although Unlimited OCR 2 enables efficient parsing of hundred-page documents, its training
data remains limited in diversity, and further data refinement will improve its robustness and
generalization. More importantly, the current sparse-attention mechanism does not preserve
global document information, limiting its understanding of document-level structures such as
heading hierarchies. Future work may introduce architectures such as linear attention to retain
global information with constant per-token computational cost, further improving document-
level structure understanding.
7. Conclusion
In this technical report, we presented Unlimited OCR 2, a next-generation architecture tailored
for unlimited-horizon document parsing. Specifically, Unlimited OCR 2 introduces Sparse
Reference Sliding Window Attention (SR-SWA) to break the long-context visual computation
bottleneck, guaranteeing constant per-token computational complexity throughout decoding.
Additionally, integrated with an optimized page-split decoding paradigm, the model signifi-
cantly mitigates GPU memory consumption, enabling robust unlimited-horizon inference on
resource-constrained hardware. Extensive evaluations and analysis confirm that Unlimited OCR
2 maintains superior parsing accuracy alongside exceptional efficiency across all scales, thereby
pioneering the OCR-3.0 era of unlimited-horizon document understanding.
